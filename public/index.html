YouTube to MP3 Converter

YTMP3 is an online YouTube to MP3 converter that lets you convert YouTube videos to MP3 or MP4 files and download them. Our service is completely free and requires no sign-up or subscription. YTMP3 works on any device — all you need is a modern web browser. By using our service, you agree to our Terms of Use.

How to use YTMP3

  1. Go to YouTube.com and find the video you want to download.
  2. Copy the video link and paste it into the converter above.
  3. Choose a format. By default, videos are converted to MP3. To switch formats, simply click the "change to …" link.
  4. Click the "Convert" button to start the conversion. Depending on the length of the video and the selected format, this may take a moment. Once the conversion is complete, click the "Download" button to save your file.
