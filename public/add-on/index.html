Add-On

Our YouTube Converter add-on makes it easy to convert YouTube videos to MP3 or MP4 files. Just choose your browser below and click the "Add to" button — you'll be redirected to the add-on's installation page.

Add to Edge Add to Firefox Add to Opera

Once the add-on is installed, you're ready to go. First, pin the add-on icon to your browser toolbar so it's always visible. Then simply go to YouTube and find the video you want to download. Open the video and click the add-on icon in your toolbar. A small pop-up will appear where you can choose the download format: MP3 (audio) or MP4 (video). Clicking a format takes you to our site and starts the conversion. After a moment, your file will be ready to download.
