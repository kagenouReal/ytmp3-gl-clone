Bookmarklets

Our bookmarklets work just like our add-on — they make it easy to download YouTube videos as MP3 or MP4 files. The advantage is that you don't need to install anything: simply drag the "YouTube to MP3" or "YouTube to MP4" button into your bookmarks bar. That's it!

Then simply go to YouTube and find the video you want to download. Open the video and click one of your bookmarklets. You'll be redirected to our site, where the conversion starts automatically. Once it's complete, you can download your file.
